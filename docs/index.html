02 / THE EVIDENCE

WHAT DID THE ODDS
GET RIGHT?

If runners are given a 20% chance, they should win about once in five starts. We compare the market’s expectations with what actually happened.

THE ODDS5.0 = $5 back on a $1 win

“Decimal odds” include your original $1. Lower odds mean the crowd thinks a win is more likely.

THE CHANCE1 ÷ 5.0 = 20%

That’s roughly one win in five tries. We adjust each race’s chances to total 100%, removing the betting pool’s built-in margin.

THE RESULTWinning once isn’t a pattern

We compare thousands of horse starts. “Favorite” means the horse with the lowest odds; “longshot” means an unlikely winner.

04 / THE TAKEAWAY

A market can identify the likeliest winners while still offering poor returns. Calibration and profitability answer different questions.

THE ANALYST’S CALL

Judge the market across many races.
Don’t build a strategy around one finish.

Behind the numbers +

A race is more than an ID

Race IDs in the original archive repeat, and many are missing. We reconstruct fields using the race catalog’s date, venue, race number, and horse list. We require all listed runners to have valid odds and numeric results, exactly one winner, at least three runners, and a plausible implied-probability total (1.05–1.50). This is a conservative complete-case sample.

What the comparison measures

For each Hong Kong runner, raw chance = 1 / decimal odds. Divide that by the sum across the race to remove the market overround. Band win rate = wins / runners. Gap = actual win rate minus normalized chance. Flat-bet ROI = total winning returns / total stakes − 1. Odds bands are lower-inclusive and upper-exclusive.

Wilson intervals describe uncertainty in pooled runner win rates, treating starts as independent. They do not account for repeated horses or all within-race dependence. The logistic calibration regression uses standard errors clustered by race.

Three tracks, two in the analysis

HV = Happy Valley; ST = Sha Tin, Hong Kong. S = Singapore in the archive’s country field; its 2002–2018 racing took place at Kranji. Singapore is excluded because the supplied odds produce inconsistent race totals. No unverified rescaling is applied.

Scope is September 2016–June 2018, when an explicit race catalog is available. Missing fields and conservative exclusions may bias the sample. The study does not represent all historical Hong Kong racing.
